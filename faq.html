    Frequently Asked Questions

    Welcome to the Token Rush FAQ! Here you'll find quick answers to the most common questions about our platform.

    What is Token Rush?

    Token Rush is a free online gaming platform where users can play a wide variety of HTML5 games directly from their web browsers. It is designed specifically for Indian gamers who want instant access to casual and competitive gaming experiences without the need to download large files or apps.

    Are the games free to play?

    Yes, all games available on Token Rush are free to play. You can enjoy our entire gaming library without any subscription fees or hidden charges.

    How do I play a game?

    Simply navigate to the "Play Games" section on our homepage, browse the available games, and click on any game card. The game will open instantly in a fullscreen in-app viewer. No downloads are required.

    Does the website require an account?

    No, you do not need to create an account to play games on Token Rush. Our platform is designed to be accessible instantly without any login or registration process.

    Are tokens or rewards available on the platform?

    Token Rush is an entertainment platform. While the name suggests "tokens," any token or point systems within specific games are strictly for in-game entertainment purposes. We do not guarantee any real-world value, cash earnings, or guaranteed winnings from these features.

    How is my user information handled?

    We respect your privacy. Since you do not need to create an account to play, we collect minimal information. We may use cookies to improve site performance and analytics. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

    Does Token Rush use cookies?

    Yes, we use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Read our Cookie Policy for more information.

    How can I report copyright content?

    If you believe that content on Token Rush infringes on your copyright, please refer to our DMCA Policy for instructions on how to submit a valid takedown notice.

    How can I contact support?

    If you have any technical issues, questions, or feedback, please visit our Contact Us page to reach out to our team directly via email or phone.
