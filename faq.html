Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing on Token Rush.

What is Token Rush?

Token Rush is a free browser gaming platform where you can discover and play online games across categories like action, arcade, puzzle, racing and sports. Pick a game, open its page and press Play Now — no account needed.

Are the games playable in a browser?

Yes. Every game on Token Rush is an HTML5 browser game that runs directly in your browser tab. There is nothing to install and nothing to configure.

Do I need to install software to play?

No. You only need a modern web browser. Open the website, choose a game and press Play Now.

How do I play a game?

Browse the collection, search, or pick a category to find a game. Click the game card to open its detail page — there you can read about it, then press Play Now to start the game.

What are "Token Rush originals"?

Originals are games we build and host directly on tokenrush.online, marked with an "Original" badge. Because they load from the same site you're on, they start faster than partner titles.

Why might a game fail to load?

Partner games are served by third-party networks, so slow connections, temporary provider issues, browser extensions or network restrictions can occasionally prevent a game from loading. Trying again, refreshing the page or switching networks usually helps. Token Rush originals load from our own servers and are the most reliable option. If a game stays broken, you can report it via our Contact Us page.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The website and its games are built for mobile browsers. Some action and racing games are best enjoyed with your phone turned sideways — the player will show a hint when that's the case.

Can I play on desktop?

Yes. Token Rush works on desktop and laptop browsers, and many games support keyboard and mouse controls. Exact controls are described on each game's detail page and inside the game itself.

How do I share a game?

Open a game's detail page and press the Share button. On phones, this opens your device's native share options; on desktop you'll get options such as Copy Link, WhatsApp, Facebook and X.

How do I copy a game link?

On any game's detail page, press the Copy URL button. It copies the game's Token Rush link (for example, tokenrush.online/#game=…) so you can paste it anywhere. Opening a shared link shows the game's detail page — the game itself only starts when Play Now is pressed.

Where does game content come from?

Token Rush originals are built and hosted on-site. Partner games come from established third-party HTML5 game networks, such as GamePix and GameMonetize, and run from their original sources. See our Disclaimer for details.

Why might a game become unavailable?

Game providers can update or remove their games at any time, which may make individual partner titles temporarily or permanently unavailable. The catalogue can also change as we refresh the collection.

Is Token Rush an earning platform?

No. Token Rush is focused on browser gaming only. It does not provide an earning, reward or referral system of any kind.
