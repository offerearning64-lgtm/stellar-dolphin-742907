1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit https://tokenrush.online/ ("Token Rush", "the website"), operated by Imran Idrisi. The Token Rush website is an entertainment platform offering free instant-play games provided by third-party game providers. This policy applies to the website only — it does not cover the separate Token Rush Android application or third-party services.

We do not require accounts, registrations or logins to use this website. We do not sell personal information. However, we do not claim that no information is ever collected: depending on how the website is accessed and which third-party services are active, certain technical information may be processed automatically, as described below.

2. Information You Provide

You can browse and play games on the website without providing any personal information. Information is only received from you when you voluntarily choose to contact us — for example, by sending an email to technicalmy75@gmail.com or calling. In that case, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. We use it solely to respond to your request and handle it no longer than needed for that purpose.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, and depending on the third-party services active at the time, certain technical information may be processed automatically when you access the website, including:

  • IP address — may be processed by hosting/infrastructure, security mechanisms and third-party services.
  • Browser and device information — such as browser type/version, device type, operating system and screen size, used for compatibility and performance.
  • Log information — standard server or service logs, such as request time and pages requested, which may be kept by hosting providers or third-party services for security and operational purposes.
  • Usage information — how pages and features are interacted with, where analytics or advertising services are active.

This information is processed by the relevant services under their own policies and settings; Token Rush does not build individual user profiles itself.

4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage or advertising identifiers, where applicable) may be used by Token Rush for functional purposes and by third parties for analytics and advertising.
