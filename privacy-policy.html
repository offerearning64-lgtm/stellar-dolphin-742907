Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025 · This policy applies to tokenrush.online.

1. Overview

Token Rush is a free browser gaming platform where visitors can discover and play online games. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the website. Token Rush does not require registration and does not operate user accounts, so we do not intentionally collect names, email addresses or other identity details through the website itself.

2. Information you provide voluntarily

The only personal information Token Rush receives is what you choose to send us yourself — for example, if you email us at technicalmy75@gmail.com or contact us through the details on our Contact Us page. Such messages may contain your email address, name and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your request and handle it in line with this policy.

3. Information handled automatically

Like virtually all websites, when you visit Token Rush your browser and network necessarily transmit certain technical information, which may be processed by our hosting infrastructure and by third-party services described below. This may include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • The pages you request and the time of the request
  • The website that referred you, where applicable

This information is used for the technical operation of the website, security and basic troubleshooting. It is not used to build marketing profiles by Token Rush itself.

4. Local storage and preferences

To remember your display preference (light or night mode), Token Rush stores a small value in your browser's local storage under the key "tr-theme". This is a functional preference saved on your own device. You can clear it at any time through your browser's site-data settings. For more detail, see our Cookie Policy.

5. Cookies

Token Rush itself does not set advertising or analytics cookies. However, cookies and similar technologies may be set by third parties whose services are integrated into the website — in particular advertising and embedded-content providers described in the sections below. These third parties process data under their own policies. See our Cookie Policy for more information.

6. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This website displays advertising served through Google AdSense to keep the games free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your settings, applicable consent, your location and Google's systems. Where consent is required, you may be presented with a choice regarding personalized advertising; you can also manage advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. Token Rush does not control and does not encourage interaction with ads; ads are clearly separate from game content and controls.

7. Third-party game providers

Games on Token Rush are provided by third-party HTML5 game networks (such as GamePix and GameMonetize) and run inside embedded frames from their original sources. When you play a game, your connection to that game is direct with the provider, and the provider may process technical information such as your IP address and device details in accordance with its own privacy policy. Token Rush does not control these providers' data practices. We recommend reviewing the relevant providers' policies if you have concerns.

8. Embedded YouTube content

The homepage may display an embedded YouTube video. YouTube is loaded only after you choose to press play. Once loaded, YouTube (Google) may collect technical data such as your IP address and may set cookies in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. If you do not interact with the video, the YouTube player is not loaded.

9. External links

Our pages may link to external websites (for example, legal references or contact tools). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

10. How information may be used

Information handled in connection with Token Rush may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure the website
  • Respond to messages you send us
  • Remember your display preferences on your device
  • Enable advertising that supports the free service
  • Address technical issues, abuse or legal requests

11. Security

We use HTTPS to protect data in transit between your browser and the website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed online. We do not operate user-account databases containing personal profiles.

12. Data retention

Messages you send us are retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and comply with any applicable obligations. Preference data (such as the theme setting) remains stored in your browser until you clear it. We do not maintain long-term profiles of visitors.

13. Children's privacy

Token Rush is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the website does not require registration, children should use the website only with the knowledge and supervision of a parent or guardian, and should never send personal information through our contact channels without permission.

14. Your choices

You can control cookies and site data through your browser settings, manage Google ad personalization via Google Ads Settings, and clear local storage at any time. If you have questions about information you have sent us, contact us at technicalmy75@gmail.com.

15. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top shows when the current version took effect. Continued use of the website after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

16. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at technicalmy75@gmail.com or via the options on our Contact Us page.
