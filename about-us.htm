About Token Rush

A simple, fast place to discover and play free browser games.

What is Token Rush?

Token Rush (tokenrush.online) is a free online gaming platform built for one purpose: helping you discover and play fun browser games without any hassle. There is nothing to install and no account needed — pick a game, open its page, and press Play Now.

What we offer

  • Game discovery — a curated collection of HTML5 games across categories such as action, arcade, puzzle, racing, sports and strategy.
  • Game detail pages — every game has its own page with a description, how-to-play notes, controls and related games, so you know what you're getting into before you start.
  • Direct browser gameplay — games load instantly in your browser tab on both mobile and desktop.
  • Mobile-friendly experience — the whole platform is designed first for phones, and works just as well on tablets and desktops.
  • Simple, clean design — games first, no clutter.

Where the games come from

Games on Token Rush are provided by established third-party HTML5 game networks and run from their original sources. Token Rush does not develop or own these games — we build the platform that makes them easy to discover and play. See our Disclaimer and Terms & Conditions for details.

Our focus

Token Rush is focused purely on browser gaming. It is not an earning, reward or referral platform — just a clean, enjoyable place to find games to play.
