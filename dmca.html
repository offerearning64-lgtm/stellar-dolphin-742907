Our Approach to Copyright

Token Rush respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of the website to do the same. We respond to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with applicable copyright principles and the process described on this page.

Third-Party Content vs Token Rush Content

Important distinction: Most games displayed on this website are provided by external third-party platforms and providers (such as GamePix and GameMonetize) through their official public game feeds, and are embedded for instant play. Token Rush does not own, develop or claim ownership of these third-party games — rights holders' concerns about a specific game are often best addressed directly to the provider that hosts it. How to Submit a Copyright Notice

If you believe that content accessible via this website infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to technicalmy75@gmail.com with the subject line "DMCA Notice". For a notice to be actionable, please include all of the following:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (for example, the title and a link or description of the original work).
  2. Identification of the allegedly infringing material — the URL(s) on https://tokenrush.online/ where the material appears, sufficient for us to locate it.
  3. Your contact information — full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement of good faith that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement of accuracy that the information in your notice is accurate and that, under penalty of perjury where applicable, you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  6. Your signature — a physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf (a typed full name in the email, together with any signature image, may serve as an electronic signature).

Incomplete notices may delay or prevent processing. Please note that knowingly submitting a false infringement claim may expose you to liability for damages, including costs and legal fees, under applicable law.

What Happens After We Receive a Notice

  • We will review the notice and, where it appears valid and within our control, remove or disable access to the identified material on this website.
  • Where the material is hosted by a third-party provider, we may additionally notify (or recommend notifying) that provider, since only they can remove the game from their platform.
  • We may forward the notice details to the affected third party where appropriate.
  • We will endeavour to acknowledge properly submitted notices within a reasonable time frame.

Counter-Notification

If you believe material identified in a takedown notice was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification to technicalmy75@gmail.com with the subject "DMCA Counter-Notice". A counter-notification should include:

  • Identification of the material that was removed and the location where it appeared before removal;
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification;
  • Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts in the operator's location, or such other jurisdiction as applicable law provides, and that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notice;
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Upon receiving a valid counter-notification, we may restore the material at our discretion, in accordance with applicable law.

Repeat Infringement

Where we have the practical ability to identify them, we reserve the right to restrict access for users who repeatedly submit abusive or fraudulent notices or counter-notices.
