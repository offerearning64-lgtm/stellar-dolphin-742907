    What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit them. They allow the website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences or login state.

    Types of Cookies We Use

    We use the following types of cookies to operate and improve our Service:

    Essential Cookies

    These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function correctly. They enable core functionalities such as security, network management, and accessibility. The Service cannot function properly without these cookies.

    Preference Cookies

    These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

    Analytics Cookies

    We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. This helps us improve the user experience by analyzing data such as which pages are visited most frequently and identifying any usability issues.

    Advertising Cookies

    These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements on other sites.

    If we use third-party vendors (such as Google AdSense) for serving ads, these vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or Google's Ads Settings.

    In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies. For example, when users play embedded HTML5 games, the game developers may set their own cookies to remember game progress or settings. We do not have control over these third-party cookies.

    Managing Cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by adjusting your browser settings.

    • Browser Controls: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can usually block all cookies, accept only essential cookies, or delete existing cookies.
    • Opt-Out Tools: You can use industry opt-out tools to manage personalized advertising, such as the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page.
