What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, phone or tablet) through your browser. They are widely used to make websites work, remember preferences, measure usage, and support features like advertising. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels and, where applicable, advertising identifiers — work in comparable ways and are covered by this policy alongside cookies.

How to Manage Cookies

  • Browser controls: all major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, Samsung Internet and others) let you view, block, delete or restrict cookies — usually under Settings → Privacy or Settings → Site Data.
  • Clearing cookies: you can delete cookies already stored on your device at any time via your browser settings.
  • Private/incognito browsing: cookies set during private sessions are typically removed when the session ends.
  • Mobile advertising identifiers: on Android and iOS devices you can limit or reset advertising identifiers in the device privacy settings.
  • Ad personalization opt-outs: where offered by providers (such as Google Ad Settings or industry opt-out pages), you can opt out of personalized advertising.

Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies will not stop you from browsing Token Rush or playing games in most cases, but some things may not work as intended — for example, some embedded games or videos may fail to load correctly, preferences may not be remembered, advertising may become less relevant, and providers may not be able to apply your previous choices. Blocking cookies does not remove advertising entirely; it may simply make ads less personalized.
